New Orleans Bachelorette Weekend Itinerary

Three days, a few great anchors and enough empty space for New Orleans to improve the plan.

WHERE TO STAYRESTAURANTSCOCKTAIL BARSLIVE MUSICITINERARY

A flexible three-day New Orleans bachelorette itinerary.

Use the plan as an anchor, not a minute-by-minute schedule. The best weekend reserves the hard things early—your stay, one signature dinner and one signature experience—then leaves white space for a courtyard cocktail, a better band or a spontaneous detour.

FRIDAY · ARRIVAL

Check in, regroup, go somewhere beautiful.

Get everyone settled, have the first cocktail at the house or a polished bar, then make dinner the centerpiece. Keep the first night close enough that travel fatigue does not kill the mood.

SATURDAY · THE BIG DAY

Brunch, one signature experience, headline dinner.

Start slower than you think, choose one daytime New Orleans experience, reset before dinner, then go to Frenchmen Street or the nightlife plan your group actually wants.

SUNDAY · RECOVERY

Jazz brunch, beignets and a neighborhood wander.

Leave room for Commander’s Palace or another brunch reservation, Café du Monde, Magazine Street or a final cocktail before departures.

THE RULE

Do not schedule every hour.

New Orleans rewards the gap between reservations. A weekend that can change when you hear a better band or find a beautiful courtyard usually feels more memorable than one built like a conference agenda.
