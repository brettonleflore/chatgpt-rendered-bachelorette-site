Best Luxury Hotels in New Orleans for a Bachelorette Weekend

For groups that want spa time, suites, Champagne at the hotel bar and the ease of full-service hospitality, these are the New Orleans luxury hotels worth comparing first.

New Orleans luxury hotels: how to choose.

There is no permanent “most expensive hotel” because rates are dynamic. Mardi Gras, Jazz Fest, Saints weekends, conventions, holidays and suite inventory can completely reorder the list. Instead of chasing a single price ranking, compare the city's top luxury tier against the experience your group actually wants.

MISSISSIPPI RIVER · EDGE OF THE FRENCH QUARTER

Four Seasons Hotel New Orleans

The modern five-star splurge: river and skyline views, a resort-style pool, spa, destination dining and Chandelier Bar. A strong choice when the group wants full-service luxury without giving up easy access to the French Quarter.

BEST FOR: MODERN FIVE-STAR LUXURY ↗ CANAL STREET · FRENCH QUARTER EDGE

The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans

Grand, traditional New Orleans luxury in the historic Maison Blanche building, with a major spa, Club Level, suites and a polished address just off the French Quarter.

BEST FOR: CLASSIC GRAND-HOTEL ENERGY ↗ CENTRAL BUSINESS DISTRICT · NEAR THE QUARTER

The Windsor Court

An old-school luxury favorite known for spacious rooms and suites, The Grill Room, Polo Club and a full-service spa. Particularly good for groups that want quieter, residential-feeling luxury near the action.

BEST FOR: SUITES + QUIET LUXURY ↗ WAREHOUSE DISTRICT · BOUTIQUE LUXURY

Maison de la Luz

A highly designed guest house with a more intimate, fashion-forward mood than a traditional large hotel. A compelling option for a smaller group that values interiors, privacy and an editorial feel.

BEST FOR: DESIGN-OBSESSED GROUPS ↗ LOWER GARDEN DISTRICT · MAGAZINE STREET

Hotel Saint Vincent

A restored 1861 property with 75 rooms, an elegant pool and courtyard, San Lorenzo, Paradise Lounge and guest-only Chapel Club. Strong for groups that want the hotel itself to feel like part of the itinerary.

BEST FOR: POOL + BOUTIQUE SCENE ↗

Private group stay vs. five-star hotel

If everyone wants to gather in one beautiful home, get ready together and have private common space, compare the hotels above with Maison Saint LaFleur. If the group values room service, separate bedrooms, spa appointments, a pool and daily hotel service, a luxury hotel may be the better fit.
