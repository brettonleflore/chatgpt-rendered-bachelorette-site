Frenchmen Street Live Music Guide

Follow the sound, not a checklist. Here is how to make live music part of a New Orleans bachelorette weekend without over-scheduling it.

Frenchmen works better as a crawl than a checklist.

Arrive early enough to hear a first set, walk the street, look into the rooms and stay where the band is right. The clubs are close enough that the night can evolve without a rigid schedule. For an iconic music room beyond Frenchmen, check Tipitina’s calendar and build the night around the show.
