NEW ORLEANS BACHELORETTE GUIDE

Things to Do on a New Orleans Bachelorette Trip

The best weekends mix iconic New Orleans moments with enough variety that every day does not feel like the same party.

WHERE TO STAYRESTAURANTSCOCKTAIL BARSLIVE MUSICITINERARY

Build the weekend around more than one entertainment corridor.

See Bourbon Street, but mix it with live music, beignets, neighborhoods, a second line, architecture, shopping, cocktails and a dinner worth dressing for. The city feels much richer when each part of the weekend has a different texture.

ONLY-IN-NEW-ORLEANS

Second line experience

A celebratory second line gives the group an unmistakably New Orleans moment and photographs beautifully when planned responsibly.

LIVE MUSIC

Frenchmen Street

Walk the clubs, listen from the door and stay where the band is right. Do not turn it into a rigid venue checklist.

ICONIC FOOD

Beignets at Café du Monde

Do the powdered-sugar ritual, preferably when the schedule has enough white space that nobody is rushing to the next reservation.

NEIGHBORHOODS

Magazine Street + Garden District

Use an afternoon for boutiques, architecture and a calmer side of the city before the evening ramps back up.

AT THE HOUSE

Private cooking or cocktail experience

If the group stays together, an in-house Creole cooking or cocktail experience can turn the home base into part of the itinerary.

AFTER DARK

Bourbon, then keep going

See it, take the photo and enjoy the spectacle—but do not make the entire weekend one street.
